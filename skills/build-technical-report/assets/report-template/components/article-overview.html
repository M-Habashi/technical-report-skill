Operating condition changes the response—but not uniformly

Ask one falsifiable question

Can a single design preserve tracking as the operating coordinate moves from condition A to condition B without exceeding the actuator command?

What the report knows

Two endpoints show a repeatable shift in resonance, phase, and transient damping.

What it does not know

Two points do not prove that the physical family is linear between them.
